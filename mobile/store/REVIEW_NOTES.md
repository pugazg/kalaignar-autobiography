# App Review notes (draft)

For the "Notes" field when submitting for review. Keep it short and useful to a reviewer.

```
Nenjukku Neethi is a native reading app for an archival Tamil memoir (the six-volume memoir of
M. Karunanidhi, 1924–2005) and his Murasoli letters. It is an independent, non-commercial digital
edition.

- No account or login is required; no sign-up, no credentials needed to use any feature.
- No in-app purchases, subscriptions, or payments.
- The app reads content over HTTPS from nenjukkuneethi.org and caches it for offline reading.
  It collects no personal data; all bookmarks/progress/preferences are stored on-device.
- No special configuration is needed to review. Launch the app and browse: Library → a volume →
  a chapter opens the reader (Tamil, with an English toggle where a translation exists). Search,
  Timeline, Explore (Themes/People/Places) and the Murasoli letters are reachable from the tabs.
- Content is historical/political prose; there is no graphic violence, sexual content, profanity,
  gambling, or user-generated content.
- The app supports iPhone and iPad. (If iPad is out of scope for this submission, that is noted
  separately — see the submission checklist.)

Support: https://nenjukkuneethi.org/support
Privacy: https://nenjukkuneethi.org/privacy
```

Do **not** add development/debug history or compliance guarantees. Update the iPad line to match
the final tablet decision (see `SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md`).
