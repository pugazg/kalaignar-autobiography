Key passages referenced by the deliverables (paragraph → gist):

- **¶0** — Opening self-question ("வாழ்க்கை வரலாறு எழுதுகிற அளவுக்கு நீ என்ன அவ்வளவு பெரிய
  மனிதனாகி விட்டாய்?"); asked by his own heart, which also held the answer. Examples of
  "ordinary" people carrying their own histories (prisoners, rickshaw-pullers, sadhus, a
  dying patient replaying life "like a film").
- **¶1** — Even an ordinary person shares, unknowingly, in the nation's changes; the
  "verdict" in question-form: "பெரிய மனிதர்களுக்குத்தான் வாழ்க்கையும் வரலாறும் சொந்தமா?
  சின்னவர்களுக்குக் கிடையாதா?" Birthday celebrated on **June 3**. Cites **Rousseau**
  ("என்னுடைய முதல் துன்பம் மனிதனாகப் பிறந்ததுதான்").
- **¶2** — Differs from Rousseau's gloom (as **Voltaire** did); lists days that give him joy
  (birth, marriage, first meeting his leader, first imprisonment in the language struggle).
  Declares **1924** a precisely consequential year; historians had begun to read the century.
- **¶3** — 1924 world/Indian setting: end of **World War I**; colonial peoples rising;
  feudal/zamindari order dying; South India's social-justice stirrings; **Arignar Anna** a
  ~15-year-old schoolboy; Gandhi's arrival energising politics; Congress split on "பூரண
  சுயராஜ்யமா? கட்டுப்பாட்டுக்கு உட்பட்ட சுயாட்சியா?"
- **¶4** — Names (Lajpat Rai, Subhas Chandra Bose, Satyamurthy, Srinivasa Iyer, C.R. Das,
  Motilal) in dispute with Gandhi; Gandhi's **only** official Congress presidency = 1924
  (Belgaum); 1924 plan made **E.V. Ramasamy** the "Vaikom hero"; **Thiru. Vi. Ka.** rises;
  Tamil labour movement roots; **Chauri Chaura** grouped with "this year" *(see review note —
  historically 1922)*; Gandhi's **Feb 1924 release** by Viceroy Reading (health) after 2 of
  6 years; young **Nehru** becomes Congress general secretary "in his 35th year."
- **¶5** — 1924 as watershed: **Kemal Pasha's** reforms; **Lenin's death**; Trotskyism
  receding, **Stalin's** rise; **Hitler** writing *Mein Kampf* in a Bavarian prison.
- **¶6** — The idea of strong regional leaders for Indian democracy; "இத்தகு கலவையான
  சூழ்நிலைகளூடே வழி நடந்த 1924-ல் நான் பிறந்தேன்"; the **1924 Mysore–Madras Cauvery
  agreement** (50-year term, review due 1974) and the contemporary **Hemavathi** dispute;
  the "ராஜா / மந்திரி" reflection; birthplace **Thirukkuvalai / Thirukkolili**, Thanjavur
  district, 15 miles from **Thiruvarur**; the tank, grove, and temples he remembers.
