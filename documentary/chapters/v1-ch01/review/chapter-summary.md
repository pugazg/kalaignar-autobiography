# Deliverable 1–3 · Summary, Timeline, Entities — v1-ch01 · பிறந்த ஆண்டு

## 1. Concise chapter summary

"பிறந்த ஆண்டு" is the opening chapter of *Nenjukku Neethi*. It is a **reflective essay,
not a narrative of events.** Karunanidhi begins by asking himself whether an ordinary life
is worth writing as history, and answers that history is not the property of "great" people
alone — every person carries a life-story, and every individual is, knowingly or not, a part
of the changes of their age. Having "granted his heart its justice," he turns to the year he
was born, **1924**, which he calls one of the century's most consequential and "question-mark"
years. He surveys what that year meant in the world and in Tamil society — the aftermath of
the First World War, colonial peoples stirring, Gandhi's release and his single official
Congress presidency, Lenin's death, the rise of Stalin, Hitler writing *Mein Kampf*, Kemal's
reforms — and, closer home, the awakening of the self-respect and social-justice movements
and the **1924 Mysore–Madras Cauvery agreement**. He notes the irony that the child born the
year of that water accord would one day, as Chief Minister, resist a dam threatening it. The
chapter closes by turning to his birthplace, the Thanjavur-district village of
**Thirukkuvalai (Thirukkolili)**, and promising a note on his parents.

**Tone:** literary, ironic, humane, politically aware. **Theme:** an individual life is
inseparable from the history, society, language, and politics of its era.
