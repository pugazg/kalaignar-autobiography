# Visual-generation prompts — opening proof (Higgsfield / image-video model)

**Rules baked into every prompt:** 16:9, 1920×1080; restrained documentary mood; warm natural
light; **no human faces / no identifiable persons**; **no text or lettering of any language**
(Tamil titles are composited later in the editor); no modern objects unless stated; not
photoreal-archival — clearly a stylised/illustrative image; no logos. Each generated
historical-looking clip will carry the on-screen label
"காட்சிப்படுத்தலுக்கான கற்பனை மறுவடிவம் · Illustrative reconstruction" **in the edit** (not in
the model).

Each clip ~5–7 s, subtle motion, seed recorded for reproducibility.

## Test A — Opening texture (→ scene P1)
```
Extreme close, slow cinematic drift across aged handmade paper with a subtle manuscript grain
and faint fibre texture; a single drop of black ink slowly spreading and darkening in the
lower third; warm low-key natural light, shallow depth of field, calm and reverent documentary
mood; no text, no letters, no symbols; no people; 16:9, filmic, gentle grain.
Negative: lettering, typography, watermark, logo, modern objects, faces, hands, harsh color.
```
Camera/motion: 5–7 s, very slow push-in / lateral drift. Purpose: title backplate.

## Test B — Historical transition to 1924 (→ scene P4)
```
Abstract archival transition mood: layered textures of blank aged newsprint and a plain
tear-off calendar page, muted sepia-and-ink palette, a faint horizontal timeline suggested by
soft light; historically neutral, understated; no readable text, no headlines, no dates baked
in, no faces, no flags, no identifiable places; 16:9, soft grain, slow cross-dissolve feel.
Negative: any legible text/number, invented newspaper headline, portrait, flag, logo, people.
```
Camera/motion: 5–7 s, slow dissolve / drift. Purpose: neutral bridge; "1924" added in edit.

## Test C — Thirukkuvalai delta landscape (→ scene P5)
```
Calm early-morning Tamil Nadu delta village landscape, illustrative not photographic: green
paddy fields, a narrow earthen road, a temple tank with still water, distant traditional
tiled-roof houses and coconut palms under soft dawn mist; peaceful, timeless, no prominent
people, no vehicles, no electric poles or modern objects; warm gentle light; 16:9, painterly
documentary style.
Negative: identifiable persons, close-up faces, modern buildings/cars/wires, text, signage,
North-Indian architecture, dramatic sky.
```
Camera/motion: 5–7 s, slow parallax over fields. Purpose: symbolic birthplace glimpse.
**Must carry the illustrative-reconstruction label in the edit.**
