# Storyboard · v1-ch01 · பிறந்த ஆண்டு

**Status:** preproduction / no images generated in this branch  
**Format:** 1920×1080 · 16:9 · 25 fps  
**Narration:** existing Tamil Chapter 1 script  
**Visual approach:** AI-assisted cinematic stills + maps/documents/typography, assembled with restrained motion in edit

## Storyboard grammar

Each storyboard shot has a stable ID (`SB-001` onward) so later generated images can be reviewed and replaced without losing continuity.

### Evidence classes
- **[TEXT]** directly anchored in the chapter/narration.
- **[HISTORY]** external context already present in the script.
- **[INTERPRETATION]** visual metaphor, graphic bridge or editorial device.
- **[RECONSTRUCTION]** clearly illustrative reconstruction, never archival evidence.

### Image-generation rule
AI models should create **image content only**. Do not ask the model to typeset exact Tamil quotations, dates or labels. Reserve clean negative space and add all exact text in post.

### Global negative prompt
`no fake archival photograph, no political campaign poster, no rally spectacle, no North Indian palace aesthetics, no fantasy temple, no neon cyberpunk, no modern cars, no modern signage, no contemporary clothing in period reconstructions, no watermark, no gibberish text, no generated Tamil lettering, no distorted hands, no duplicate people, no melodramatic hero pose`

# Cross-shot continuity checklist

## Kalaignar identity
- Master visual identity: SB-002.
- Match facial proportions, glasses shape, age treatment and skin tone in SB-004, SB-018 and SB-022.
- Do not let image generation drift into a generic Tamil politician or younger/older inconsistent likeness.
- Avoid generated speaking-mouth frames; narration remains voice-over.

## Period geography
- Thirukkuvalai plates SB-019/020 must feel like Cauvery-delta Tamil Nadu, not Kerala, North India or a fantasy heritage set.
- The temple is supporting geography, not a monumental spectacle.
- Village life must remain plausible for the early twentieth century: no modern utility poles, vehicles, plastic objects or contemporary signage unless historically verified.

## Maps
- AI can generate texture and cartographic style, but geographic accuracy should be checked and final labels / river paths redrawn in post where precision matters.
- Cauvery path is a factual element; do not accept hallucinated tributaries simply because an image looks attractive.

## Tamil typography
- All exact Tamil titles, quotations, dates, labels and source URLs are **post-production typography**, not part of AI still generation.
- Use a verified Tamil-capable layout engine and approved font.

## Reconstruction labels
Shots SB-015, SB-019 and SB-020 are explicit reconstructions. If the final visual could be mistaken for a genuine historical photograph, add:

**காட்சிப்படுத்தலுக்கான கற்பனை மறுவடிவம் · Illustrative reconstruction.**

## Review gate before image generation
Before generating final-quality plates, approve:
1. SB-002 Kalaignar master portrait direction.
2. SB-019 Thirukkuvalai master environment.
3. SB-016 Cauvery map style.
4. SB-011/012 historical-figure illustration style.
5. Global paper / teal / brass palette.
