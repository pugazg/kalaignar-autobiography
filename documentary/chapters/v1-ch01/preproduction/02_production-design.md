# Production design · v1-ch01 · பிறந்த ஆண்டு

**Status:** preproduction / image generation not started  
**Purpose:** define the repeatable visual world before any AI storyboard or final plate is rendered.

The design method is continuity-led: every location, portrait, map, prop and texture receives a stable production-design ID so later image generation can be compared against the same visual bible.

---

# 1 · GLOBAL VISUAL BIBLE

## PD-G01 · Core tone
- dignified
- reflective
- literary
- historically responsible
- emotionally warm without sentimentality
- political history without campaign aesthetics

The documentary should feel closer to a museum exhibition, literary biography and memory essay than to a political advertisement.

## PD-G02 · Palette
- **Paper:** `#FAF7F1`
- **Ink:** `#0F1720`
- **Marina teal:** `#0E5D63`
- **Antique brass:** `#B98A2F`
- **Muted sepia:** supporting historical tone only
- **Charcoal grey:** maps, shadows, captions

Rules:
- teal should function as a quiet continuity colour, especially for Cauvery / maps / section rules.
- brass is an accent, not a gold-glamour effect.
- avoid heavy orange-teal cinema grading.

## PD-G03 · Surface language
Primary materials:
- uncoated book paper
- aged but clean official paper
- fountain-pen ink
- understated newspaper texture
- hand-drawn cartography
- linen / cotton / simple wood when an environment needs tactile reality

Avoid:
- fake burned paper edges
- exaggerated scratches
- sepia on every shot
- “old movie” damage overlays that imply false archival provenance

## PD-G04 · Typography
- Exact Tamil is always set in post.
- Never trust generated Tamil lettering for source quotations.
- Maintain generous line spacing and avoid filling the image with text.
- Primary title style: literary serif Tamil.
- Dates / map labels may use a restrained supporting sans / serif system.
- Keep English secondary.

## PD-G05 · AI image truthfulness
Each generated image belongs to one of three categories:
1. **Editorial portrait / collage** — contemporary editorial treatment using a known public figure identity.
2. **Historical illustration** — identifiable historical figure or context represented as an illustration, not fake evidence.
3. **Illustrative reconstruction** — recreated place or period environment.

If a reconstruction looks photographic enough to be mistaken for evidence, label it in the final film.

## PD-G06 · Camera language
- predominantly eye-level and human-scale
- portrait focal lengths: 70–100mm
- environment focal lengths: 28–40mm
- maps / documents: overhead or near-overhead
- motion added later should stay subtle: 2–5% push, drift or parallax
- avoid drone-like impossible travel through historical reconstructions

## PD-G07 · Lighting language
- motivated natural light
- warm desk / paper pools for writing sequences
- humid dawn light for Thirukkuvalai
- neutral museum daylight for maps / history
- no hard “hero rim” or stage spotlight for Kalaignar

## PD-G08 · Sound-aware design
Although this document concerns visuals, shots should leave room for the existing audio grammar:
- paper settling / page turn
- fountain pen
- distant press-room hum
- water ambience
- birds / village morning
- restrained solo instrument / tonal bed

Visuals should not be so busy that they compete with narration.

# 2 · CHARACTER / PORTRAIT BIBLE

## PD-C01 · Kalaignar master identity
**Use in:** Scenes 01, 02, 06, 08, 09

### Required identifiers
- M. Karunanidhi / Kalaignar
- iconic dark glasses
- mature / elderly visual identity for editorial portrait shots
- composed writer-statesman presence
- no imitation speech performance

### Expression range
- opening: calm authority
- inner question: reflective / inward
- Cauvery: thoughtful, lightly ironic rather than triumphant
- closing: resolved and contemplative

### Wardrobe
Prefer an editorially neutral treatment derived from the approved/reference portrait available to the production. Do not allow AI to invent ornate ceremonial costume.

### Continuity control
The first approved Kalaignar image becomes the **master face reference**. All later generated portrait shots must be image-to-image / reference-conditioned against that master where tooling permits.

### Avoid
- rally pose
- raised hand or fist
- huge cheering crowd
- party flag as background wallpaper
- excessive orange/yellow political-poster palette
- face drift across shots
- uncanny over-smoothing

# SCENE 07 · THIRUKKUVALAI

This is the principal environment-design scene and should receive the strongest location research before final image generation.

## PD-L01 · Thirukkuvalai memory-world
- **Source class:** [TEXT] + [RECONSTRUCTION]
- **Narrative anchors from chapter:** temple tank, surrounding grove, Shiva temple.
- **Period target:** early twentieth-century village memory, not present-day exact reconstruction unless current references are intentionally used.

### Geography
- Cauvery-delta humidity and vegetation.
- Tank should read as village water / temple geography, not ornamental palace lake.
- Temple positioned as part of settlement fabric rather than dominating as fantasy monument.

### Architecture
- modest tiled / vernacular houses where visible
- stone tank steps
- temple form consistent with Tamil Shaiva village architecture
- no Kerala-only sloping-roof visual shorthand
- no giant fantasy gopuram unless verified for the site and period

### Vegetation
- layered tropical / delta grove
- avoid manicured resort landscaping
- natural density around water

### Ground / water
- worn stone
- damp earth
- calm reflective water with light organic debris acceptable
- no turquoise resort water

### Lighting
**Master look:** humid dawn moving into first warm sunlight.

### Human presence
Optional tiny background figures only if composition needs scale. Do not stage invented childhood-Kalaignar action unless separately sourced.

### Reconstruction label
Required if the final scene could be mistaken for an authentic historical photograph.

## PD-L01-A · Wide establishing plate
- 28mm wide
- slightly elevated
- temple tank central
- grove frames edges
- temple distant

## PD-L01-B · Water-level plate
- 40mm
- low perspective across reflections
- stone steps foreground
- temple / grove background

## PD-L01-C · Memory dissolve plate
- environment merges into paper texture
- central text-safe field
- no additional props

# 5 · CONTINUITY MATRIX

## Portrait continuity
- one approved Kalaignar master reference drives all portrait generations.
- dark glasses shape and face geometry must not drift.
- skin tone and age remain consistent.
- no sudden wardrobe changes unless intentional and sourced.

## Map continuity
- same cream / charcoal / teal cartographic family across world, Tamil region and Cauvery.
- brass markers use same size and edge treatment.
- all labels added later with one typography system.

## Paper continuity
- retain one master texture family; do not use a different “vintage paper” preset in every scene.
- paper aging remains subtle.

## Reconstruction continuity
- Thirukkuvalai frames must match weather, season, vegetation and architecture.
- SB-019 and SB-020 should feel like two camera positions in the same place, same morning, not separate AI worlds.

## Historical illustration continuity
- Gandhi and Lenin must share rendering style, paper integration and contrast level.

---

# 6 · WHAT MUST NOT BE GENERATED

Do not generate:
- exact Tamil quotations inside images
- fake source pages
- fake government agreements
- fake archival newspaper headlines
- fake signatures / seals
- childhood Kalaignar photograph
- exact young-Annadurai face presented as fact
- fabricated historical crowd events
- political party propaganda composition

Where exact source material exists later, composite the verified scan / document instead.
