Archival scene 1

(Inside the cave, an invisible figure is sculpting a statue, smiling before it.)

பெரியவர்Victory... the reward won by one who heeds not pain!... A fitting harvest for the suffering of so many years! From now on, I shall accomplish all that I have set my mind on... Kesari Varma! Let joy overflow in those eyes clouded by sorrow. Let the clouds of grief flee far from you. Troubles—henceforth, dust to us...... Ha... ha... ha... Victory; victory;

Only Kesari Varman's voice is heard. The chisel and hammer keep at their work.

கேசரி வர்மன்Gurudeva! Is it true? Can your eyes not see me!......

பெரிYes, Kesari Varma! You have become invisible. The medicine I discovered has begun to work. What could not be attained has been attained. A magnificent triumph for my research. You can see everyone. But no one can see you.

கேசரிGurudeva!... Thirteen years... I have burned with one thought—to avenge myself upon that sinner! Now that you have shown me the path at last! ......Swami! Will this invisibility not last long?......

பெரிIt will not—but we can try further. If I find the herbs I seek, you may not merely remain invisible; you may even become imperishable. But Kesari Varma! Use this so that the good may live!

கேசரிI will use it so the good may live! At the same time, I will expose this destroyer's fox-like tricks. Sinner! Vindictive wretch................... Bhadrakali who poisoned her own husband! Your poison did not kill me, woman. Kesari Varman is alive! He has not died—he will not die, not before exposing your plot! Cheat!.....glib-tongued trickster!

பெரியHa ha ha............

கேசரிGuruji.

பெரிIt's the statue, my boy!

கேசரிA statue, yes! Why am I sculpting her? To rehearse the torture; Vasantha Sena! I will thrust a spear into your eyes—set your hair aflame—those kovvai-red lips!... I will drive sharp needles into them..... Your mirror-bright cheeks! Ha ha ha... I will slash them with a dagger... that body that drives onlookers mad... I will hack it into pieces and fling them away! The wondrous work of invisibility has begun, woman!... Enchantress, the chapter of vengeance by your former husband has begun. Walking serpent!... Dance of destruction itself! Treacherous demon... gnawing spider.........

Archival scene 2

The scene opens in the palace with Vasantha Sena's laughter.

வசந்தசேனைMad girl! Even now, if I choose, I can become the chief queen!......

தோழிThat is my wish too, amma. But while the Maharani and the prince are still alive...

வசMaharani—the prince... both are living corpses. I need only laugh, and the throne itself is mine. One bewitching glance—and this whole realm lies at my feet! I am the queen of the Maharaja's love, girl! I need only will it—Vasantha Sena, queen! Her son Vasanthan, prince!

Archival scene 3

In one part of the palace.

வசந்தன்Yes, yes—no doubt, Vikata! The king's son is the prince! About that, I have no doubt...

விகடன்Vasantha! Is that the only thing we have no doubt about?... On any day, in anything, there is not even the slightest bit of doubt in us.

(Vasanthan and Vikatan — the ‘No Doubt’ song.)

Archival scene 4

In another part of the palace.

பத்மாவதி(Before the King's portrait) Swami! Will the day never come when the two of us are united again...? In all my life I have done no harm to anyone! I have forgiven even Vasantha Sena, who ruined my life.... I did so because your deep affection for her should not bring you pain!... And even if I had harmed someone, have I not suffered for many years, wasting away apart from you? Is this punishment not enough? Swami! The birthday of our darling Manoharan is approaching too. Shall we not both kiss the crown of our beautiful, blooming treasure and bless him together—can that day of joy never come again, Swami!
