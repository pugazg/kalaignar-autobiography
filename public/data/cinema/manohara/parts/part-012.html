மனோகரன்Vijaya! Suicide is cowardice!

விஜயாEntering the women's inner chamber in the dark is greater cowardice still...

மனோகரன்Never mind. I have saved a flowering creeper from the storm of death.

விஜயாNo one need stop the death of this slave.

மனோகரன்Slave? Who is a slave? You are a princess made of beauty itself...

(Rajapriyan enters.)

ராஜThat is exactly why I say one must not enter the inner chamber at midnight!

மனோRajapriya! Do you know the news?

ராஜI came knowing your news, didn't I! Manohara! This is a time to be on guard. Do not be deceived! A little while ago the princess was Yama himself coming to kill you!

விஜI too have a heart. Those who possess a good heart make a mistake only once.

மனோVijaya! You are one who must live. More than that, you are under my protection... I cannot permit such disastrous acts.

விஜLife is not your slave. It was given by the Lord.

மனோThe explanation for that—tomorrow, in the inquiry hall.

Archival scene 17

(The inquiry hall of the Pandiyan people.)

ராஜப்பிரியன்She tried to commit murder—she attempted suicide. She has committed enormous, unforgivable crimes, Princess! She stands before the people's assembly! The very lifetime of the young princess who tried to separate our prince, my dearest friend, the jewel of the Chola line—this Manoharan—must be brought to an end. Princess! Prepare to suffer the punishment I give you for your whole life. By sunset tomorrow evening—at the Kali temple—no! I do not wish to postpone the punishment even by one day—for the crime of Vijayadevi, tender creeper of the Pandiyan land, attempting to kill the Chola land's athaan, today itself—right now—in this very place—before everyone—you must place a garland upon Manoharan and accept him as your bridegroom!

Archival scene 18

(The Pandiyan palace.)

மனோகரன்Vijaya.... I thought you alone were the beauty in this palace. I failed to notice that there is a golden creeper to rival you.

விஜயாWho?

மனோ[Pointing to Vijaya's reflection in the mirror] There! This smile is a sadir-dance of diamond dewdrops upon a golden rose!

விஜLeave that aside! I am the enemy's daughter! Will your mother and father accept me?

மனோI wondered the same thing. I am your enemy, after all—would you accept me?

விஜI loved your valour. The soil of my motherland stopped it! I was enchanted by your charm! My father's affection dammed it back. In the end, your honest heart conquered me! What?

மனோSpeak, my dear... speak! I go on savouring the honey-waves of the sea of your nectar-dripping lips... the love-epics your eyes write and heap before me, queen of my heart... speak a little more...
