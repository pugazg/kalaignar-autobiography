Archival scene 15

(Night. Manoharan's camp-house.)

Bauthayanan raises a dagger to kill the sleeping Manoharan.

At that moment Vijaya enters in a soldier's dress, unseen by anyone, with a drawn sword, and without realizing it cries out, ‘Oh!’

Manoharan wakes, catches Vijaya's hand—

மனோWho are you?

விஜயாI am neither a ghost given another life in the cremation-ground you created, nor a corpse! I am one among the enemies who escaped your sight—a warrior of the Pandiyan land!

மனோA warrior of the Pandiyan land! Hmm... it seems the first lesson under Muthu Vijayan's rule is military training in lurking and pouncing at midnight, you spineless fellow. Is one of those men left who carry a sword in the hand and cowardice in the heart? Run away... save your life...

ராஜப்பிரியன்Manohara!... We must not let him go...

மனோLet the poor fellow go... I have forgiven him.

விஜOurs is not a tradition that begs forgiveness...

மனோA tradition of attacking from hiding... great warrior!

ராஜThose who cut off a sleeping man's head are warriors too—in the schools of the Pandiyan land! Get out, you worthless coward!

விஜCoward... am I a coward! Look here... I shall take my revenge openly, right now....

[She draws her sword. As Manoharan stops her, her headcloth slips away.]

மனோAh! A woman!

விஜYour enemy's daughter... the woman who came for revenge...

ராஜPrincess!

மனோA marvellous sight—a sword in hands that wear bangles!

விஜDo not talk!... If you are a warrior, speak after you defeat me!

மனோHistory has no record of warriors defeating spear-eyed women, Princess!

விஜNo need for mockery... draw your sword.

மனோMy sword plays only on the battlefield... it does not bruise fruit!

விஜIf you have courage—if all that talk about the marks of a warrior was not mere tongue-play—swing your sword... let a good judgement be delivered....

மனோA good judgement... will not be delivered by the sword-point. From now on it must be obtained in the court of law! Tender creeper born of the Pandiyan, you are now a prisoner—a prisoner under protection! Rajapriya! Take her away!

ராஜப்There is our flag at the fort gate! A flag announcing that Princess Vijaya is Manoharan's slave!

விஜயாDo not speak! I lived as a princess—I will not live as a slave. I roamed like a skylark—I will not be caught in the cruel grip of vultures. I floated in the breeze of freedom—I will not be trapped in the cyclone of slavery. The lap of death is far better than the feet of an enemy.
