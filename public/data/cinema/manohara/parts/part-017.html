Archival scene 29

Cave. The elder is absorbed in discovering a medicinal method...

பெரியவர்Before this invisibility medicine runs out, victory is certain......

கேசரிவர்மன்The invisibility medicine... has dwindled. A little more...

பெரியவர்No need...... Here...... today itself I shall discover a sanjeevi that does not die...

கேசரிவர்மன்(Pointing to Vasantha Sena's statue)... Vasantha Sena... I must tear out her heart, squeeze it, and paint this statue red... her eyes...

[An explosion...... The Gurunathar falls amid smoke. He is carried outside... his life is wavering...]

பெரியவர்Kesari Varma... my last... blessing.... Do not rush... make Manoharan your ally... for your goal!... I... I am dying......

கேசரிவர்மன்You laboured without rest to tell the world of a wonder! The invisibility medicine. You did not even show me that method... Swami! All those who discovered something new vanished without revealing it to others... You too have walked that path... is there such a curse upon this country itself?...

Archival scene 30

Song / performance reference

Spring-festival celebration. Songs... Manoharan and Vijaya sing.

விஜயாMay this love never, ever change...

மனோகரன்What kind of question is this, Vijaya!

விஜயாNo... if you too, like Uncle... were to fall under the spell of some Vasantha Sena—

மனோVasantha Sena. Do not even utter her name to me!......... That dog in the place where my mother should be......... Unless I make her writhe like a worm fallen into burning embers—and seat my mother in the royal hall... I am not Manoharan.

Vijaya starts and rises...

விஜயாAh!...

மனோWhat?

Vasanthan rises from beneath the seat.

வசந்தன்You stepped on my foot, Anni!

மனோHey, were you hiding here?

வசந்Hmm... if all the women beat you with water and flowers, water-flowers and flower-water, flower-water and water-flowers, you'd hide too!

மனோ(Laughing...) Brave man...

Vijaya also laughs......

வசந்Laugh... laugh...

Vasantha Sena approaches with the king......

வசந்திVasantha!... Vasantha!...

வசந்தன்I'm here, Amma... what, Amma...

[They come there.]

வசந்தன்Amma!... Anna and Anni are laughing at me!...
