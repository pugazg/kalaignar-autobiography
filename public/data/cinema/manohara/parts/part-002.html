Archival scene 5

(Inside the cave.)

பெரியவர்Where are you off to in such a hurry, Kesari Varma?

கேசரி வர்மன்To the play—I have arranged for those very people there.

பெரிI had forgotten. Is today Manoharan's birthday celebration? Hmm... what name have you given the play?

கேசரிThe Sword of War.

பெரிShabash... there is no obstacle to staging it, is there?...

கேசNone. Because they will not understand the story...

பெரிWhat alias have you given Vasantha Sena in your play?

கேசDeva Sena!

பெரிYour name?

கேசEeswari Varman. I have changed Purushothaman to Uthama Purushan.

பெரிThey will certainly understand the story.

கேசThey must understand it. At least then, will Purushothaman not come to his senses?

பெரிHmm... try and see!

கேசI shall go, Gurudeva!

பெரிBe careful.

Archival scene 6

(Palace. Vasantha Sena is looking at Ugrasena's portrait, hidden inside the pendant on her pearl necklace.)

தோழிAmma... whatever else you may forget day after day, you never forget this, do you? What is there in this pendant that draws your eyes like a magnet?

வசNo magnet, girl! A god who lends a hand in danger!

தோA god?

வசLook... (She shows the portrait.)

தோAmma, the Maharaja is coming!

புருஷோத்தமன்Still not finished with your adornment? It is already time for the play...

வசந்There—finished.

புருHave you forgotten? Today is Manoharan's birthday.

வசந்Would I forget—the boy's birthday? Another memory came to me...

புருஷோத்What is it, Vasanthi?

வசந்The thirteenth anniversary of our festival of love.
