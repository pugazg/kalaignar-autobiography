பத்மாAh!...

துறவிYour son has gone... to the world of merit!...

பத்மாAh... Manohara!...

[A voice is heard. Leaning on her companion, she starts and turns, unable to believe her eyes.]

‘Manohara!’

She embraces Manoharan and looks at him in astonishment...

மனோகரன்Why are you looking at me like this, Amma... what happened...? Who is this Sami?... (Seeing the war-sword in his hand) Ah... my war-sword... it went missing... how did it come here...?

பத்மாManohara! This sinner told me you had left me forever...

ராஜப்பிரியன்Thieving rogue!... Not only has he stolen the sword... has he stretched out a tail too? Look at the beard, the beard!

[When the beard is grabbed, it comes away in the hand.]

மனோகரன்Bauthayanan...

ராஜA new chapter in conspiracy—steal the war-sword and bring it to show her... the Maharani will die. A fine plot...

மனோகரன்Scorpion's sting!... Whose plan is this, you wretch!... Speak...

பத்மாManohara, poor fellow... let him go...

மனோகரன்We must not let him go, Amma... Rajapriya... lock him in prison!

பெளத்Vasantha Sena is the cause of everything!

மனோகரன்Vasantha Sena! She must not be left alive any longer!

(He goes.)

பத்மாManohara! Stop—where are you running?...

மனோகரன்Amma, what more now—after I have fulfilled your vow!...

பத்மாI'll tell you. First, ‘put the sword into its sheath!... swear that without my permission you will do no kind of harm either to the Maharaja or to Vasantha Sena!... swear upon me...

மனோAmma!

பத்மாSwear upon your mother!...

மனோI will do nothing to the king, Amma.

பத்மாNo! It must include Vasantha Sena too!

மனோVasantha Sena... the vulture circling overhead!... the wolf standing with its jaws agape!... the mountain-python that has coiled around us—mercy for her—??? Impossible! Never!...

பத்மாAs you wish... let the townspeople laugh that you are a son who does not heed his mother's word!... let the country laugh that I am the mother who bore an ungovernable son!...
