Archival scene 20

[Vasantha Sena's inner chamber.]

[Bauthayanan enters with Manoharan's war-sword.]

வசந்தாBauthayanan...

பெளத்தாயனI brought it—the war-sword!

வசந்தாShabash!...

பெளத்Ammani!... Manoharan returned victorious... and even married Vijaya...

வசந்Then...

பெளத்Listen—they held a swimming game. I made Vijaya a widow and came back with the sword.

வசந்தாManoharan is dead?...

பெளத்Certainly!... Ammani! The reward money!

வசந்Here!...

[She gives him the sack of gold.]

Bauthayanar!... one more task... two hunts with a single aim! If you tell Padmavathi this news, her heart will burst and she will die... after that... I alone am the queen!... you are the Prime Minister!...

பெளத்Prime Minister?... Then I'll do anything you want...

Archival scene 21

Padmavathi's inner chamber.

தோழி 1Hmm... weariness from war—delight with a new wife—how will he come quickly, Amma...!

பத்மாMy heart is restless with the desire to see Vijaya!...

தோழி 2Amma... some renunciant says he must see you, urgently!

(Bauthayanan enters in the guise of a renunciant.)

பத்மாGreetings...

துறவிMay you live in well-being... Maharani... first make your heart into iron...

பத்மாWhat?...

துறவிThe news I am about to utter is no sugarcane! Hmm... human life is but a straw in the sea, Amma!

பத்மாWhat, Sami; you speak without any connection.

துறவிA renunciant is one without ties, is he not!... How greatly you must have rejoiced on hearing that your son had won...

பத்மாYes, Swami!...

துறவிWhy does the same heart that embraces happiness refuse to face sorrow?
