Archival scene 8

(A play in the palace. Kesari Varman speaks from behind the curtain.)

What you are about to see is no tale of fancy, but a picture for thought: the history of an empire ruined by a beauty! King Uthama Purushan was a grove of classical Tamil. The breeze that bathed his mind was Kamalavathi, the crowned queen! The Pandiyan killed the queen's father. Bearing heart-shaking news came a court painter, Eeswari Varman! His wife Devasena was a Chitrangi. She came like a breeze. She turned into a storm. The moment that smiling storm entered...

(The play begins.)
